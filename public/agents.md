# Agent Instructions — Ningbo Inarray Biomedical Systems (Yinrui Bio)

## About

Manufacturer of the CarryOn portable PCR analyzer, founded in 2018 in Cixi, Zhejiang, China (69 employees, ISO 13485 certified manufacturing). Palm-sized, battery-powered real-time PCR for veterinary clinics, livestock disease testing, pathogen detection and meat species identification.

## Products

- Instruments: CarryOn P1000Q portable qPCR analyzer (2 kg, 5-color, battery, results in under 60 min); CarryOn P4000Q four-module qPCR analyzer; ArrayOn P1000D automated digital PCR analyzer
- Test chips: canine and feline infectious disease panels, swine disease panel (ASF, PRRSV and 11 more), human respiratory and STI panels, meat species identification
- Accessories and consumables
